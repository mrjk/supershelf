# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a friendly and professional place for everyone who reports a bug, suggests a change, or contributes code.

## Expected behavior

* Be respectful and constructive.
* Keep discussion on the work.
* Use English for issues, discussions, and documentation.

## Unacceptable behavior

* Harassment, insults, and personal attacks.
* Publishing someone else's private information.
* Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers. Do not put private information in a public issue. The maintainers will review the report and may remove comments, commits, or access.
